Privacy

Short, because there is not much to say.

No accounts, no tracking across visits

There is no sign-up and no login. We set no cookies, and we do not use any advertising identifier. We cannot recognise you when you come back, and we have not built anything that tries to.

Snake keeps your best score in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device, is never sent to us, and identifies nobody — it is a number on your own machine. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Advertising

Ads are chosen and served by us, on this domain. There are no third-party ad networks, no external trackers, and no data is shared with anyone. Ads are selected from the page you are on, your country and your device type — never from a profile of you, because we do not build one.

How long we keep it

Raw event data for 90 days, then only aggregate counts. Server logs for 7 days.

Fonts and third parties

The site loads no third-party resources at all — no font CDN, no analytics service, no embedded widgets. Everything is served from this domain, so no other company sees your visit.
